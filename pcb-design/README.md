## Board specifications
- 2 layers, FR4, 0.062" ±10%, ENIG (2 µ") surface finish, 1 oz outer copper
- Green LPI solder mask both sides, white silkscreen top only
- SMT top side only — 328 pads, 19.6 mil min pitch
- 306 holes across 10 drill sizes, smallest 9.8 mil; 7.8 mil min trace/space; tented vias
- IPC Class II, RoHS, electrical test yes, no impedance control
- Board 2.913" × 1.335", panelized 12-up on a 9.94" × 9.51" array, route-and-retain (you snap them apart)
